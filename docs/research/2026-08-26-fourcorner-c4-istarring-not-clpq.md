## The refusal (numerology vs number theory)

Four corners, four C₄ phases, four Cl(3,0) even-grade pieces if you
squint (scalar + three bivectors) — a coincidence of counts is a
**generator**, not an identification
(`.claude/rules/numerology-vs-number-theory.md`). What identifies an
object is the invariant that excludes the competitors.

| Competitor | Count of four | Excluded by |
|---|---|---|
| C₄ = `{1, i, −1, −i}` | 4 units | `i² = −1`, `i⁴ = 1` (cyclic of order 4) |
| FourCorner 2×2 I/O record | 4 slots | occupancy of options, **no** group law on the record |
| Cl(3,0) | 8-dimensional (not 4) | `eᵢ² = +1`; even subalgebra ≅ ℍ (4-dimensional over ℝ, **not** C₄) |
| Cl(0,1) ≅ ℂ | 2-real-dimensional | this **is** the algebra whose generator is `i` — related, still not FourCorner |

FourCorner is a directed 2×2 of (data × feedback) × (in × out). C₄ is
the **name** of its compass. Cl(p,q) is a geometric algebra classified
by a quadratic form. Sharing a drawing of four arrows does not make
them the same object.

## How they *are* related (three embeddings)

Checked in `src/Core/FourCornerC4.fs` and
`tests/Tests.FSharp/Formal/FourCornerC4.Laws.Tests.fs`.

### 1. Compass labeling

`FourCorner.fs` already writes **N S E W = `{1, i, −1, −i}` = C₄ =
`i`-rotation**. That is a *labeling* of the four directed corners
(TIn rest, TOut, TOutFeedback / retraction, TInFeedback co-owned).
`FourCornerOwnership` remains a product of options. It does not
multiply.

### 2. IStarRing witness — why VALUE needs a ring

`FourCornerTrace` is the Joyal–Street–Verity trace over `WSet`:
feedback moves the generator's interpretation; the VALUE ping-return
is `−gen(before, H) + gen(after, H)`. That minus is `IRing.Negate`.
`IStarRing : IRing`, so the trace's first argument is an `IStarRing`.
Inverse-free corners (Boolean, tropical, EP / ADF re-normalisation)
stay at `ISemiring` and **cannot instantiate** the trace — a
compile-time refusal, not a runtime throw (`IRing.cs`).

On ℂ (`ImaginaryStack.complex`) the compass generator and the
retraction are the same ring identity:

```text
i² = Negate(One)
```

Two quarter-turns of a weight **are** `pingReturn`. L5 already pinned
this on `WSet`; `FourCornerC4.twoQuarterTurns` pins it on the ring so
the claim does not depend on consolidation.

This is **algebra**, not physics. It is not a claim that the substrate
is quantum, nor that the trace exploits quantum mechanics. It is GDL
(Aji–McEliece 2000) over a different semiring.

### 3. Clifford discriminator — ±1 is signature, not the compass

Clifford generator squares `v² = Q(v) ∈ {+1, −1}` are the
**signature** `(p, q)` of Cl(p,q): `p` generators square to `+1`, `q`
to `−1` (Lounesto 2001). That ±1 is a property of the **quadratic
form on vectors**, not of the C₄ compass.

In-tree Cl(3,0) (`Cl3.fs`):

- `e₁² = e₂² = e₃² = +1 = One` — Euclidean signature.
- `e₁₂² = −1 = Negate(One)` — the even subalgebra `{scalar + bivectors} ≅ ℍ`.

So C₄ **does** live in Cl(3,0), but as the cyclic group generated by a
**bivector**, the way ℂ sits inside ℍ — not as a generating vector,
and not as the whole algebra.

The Clifford algebra whose generator *is* `i` is **Cl(0,1) ≅ ℂ**,
already a textbook row of `CliffordPeriodicity` (`Cl(0,1) is C`).
Cl(3,0) ≅ M₂(ℂ) is a different Morita type (matrix dimension 2, not
1). Matching “complex ground” is not identity.

The **wrong** embedding `FourCornerC4.toCl3Vector` (send compass `i`
to `e₁`) is the falsifier: `e₁² = +1 ≠ Negate(One)`. If a later slice
claims “the four corners are Cl(3,0)”, that test goes red.

## Composition is not identification

`Cl3.algebra : IStarRing<Mv>` (reversion as `Conj`, geometric product
as `Mul`, `Negate = smul −1`). Because the TRACE consumes `IStarRing`
and not a Clifford signature, `FourCornerTrace.start/step` instantiates
over `Cl3.Mv` **weights**. That is the same generic circuit with a
different ring — the GDL fact again. It does not make the I/O record
into a geometric algebra.

Sibling honesty already on file: ISR Kleisli `>=>` is the interrupt
**error** channel and has no Negate (`081M10AZ6KS087G0R0000SSFMH`);
EP/ADF is re-normalisation, not Z-set minus.

## Existing IStarRing instances — which apply

Aaron 2026-08-26 follow-up: we already have several `IStarRing`
dictionaries. They **do** apply, but TRACE and C₄ are different asks.

| Instance | TRACE (`Negate` / ping-return) | C₄ generator `u² = −1` |
|---|---|---|
| `IntegerRing.Star` (ℤ, Conj=id lift of existing `IRing`) | **yes** | **no** — unit group `{±1} ≅ C₂` |
| `Real.algebra` | **yes** | **no** — same C₂ |
| `ImaginaryStack.complex` | **yes** | **yes** (`i`) |
| `ImaginaryStack.quaternion` | **yes** | **yes** — `{1,i,−1,−i}` and `{1,j,−1,−j}` are **subgroups** of ℍ*; i and j anticommute, so ℍ is not C₄ |
| `ImaginaryStack.octonion` | **yes** | **yes** (any imaginary unit); Mul is non-associative above ℍ |
| `ImaginaryStack.sedenion` | **yes** | **yes**; also zero divisors |
| `Cl3.algebra` | **yes** | **even subalgebra** `e₁₂`, not vectors (`eᵢ² = +1`) |
| `IntervalRing` | **no** — `ISemiring` only (not even `IRing`; Moore arithmetic has no additive inverse) | n/a |
| Tropical / Boolean / EP | **no** — inverse-free | n/a |
| TS `star-ring.ts` / `bayesian/wset.ts` `RealAlgebra` | ports of ℝ/ℂ/doubling | same split |

| Python `StarRing` | abstract class, no instance | — |
| C# `IntegerRing` | `IRing` only (no `Conj`) | TRACE via `IRing`; `WSet` still wants `IStarRing` |

`Doubled.algebra` is the lift, not a fourth algebra: it is how ℂ/ℍ/𝕆/𝕊
are obtained from ℝ. `Chip8Cow` / `QubitIso` are explicitly **not**
`IStarRing` (no natural state×state product).

Law packs under `tests/` still re-box a local `intStar` (`Conj = id`).
`IntegerRing.Star` is now the named src instance; switching the packs
is remaining hygiene, not a new ring.
