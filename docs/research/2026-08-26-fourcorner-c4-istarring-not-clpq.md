## The refusal (numerology vs number theory)

Four corners, four C₄ phases, four Cl(3,0) even-grade pieces if you
squint (scalar + three bivectors) — a coincidence of counts is a
**generator**, not an identification
(`.claude/rules/numerology-vs-number-theory.md`). What identifies an
object is the invariant that excludes the competitors.

| Competitor | Count of four | Excluded by |
|---|---|---|
| C₄ = `{1, i, −1, −i}` | 4 units | `i² = −1`, `i⁴ = 1` (cyclic of order 4) |
| FourCorner 2×2 I/O record | 4 slots | occupancy of options, **no** group law on the record |
| Cl(3,0) | 8-dimensional (not 4) | `eᵢ² = +1`; even subalgebra ≅ ℍ (4-dimensional over ℝ, **not** C₄) |
| Cl(0,1) ≅ ℂ | 2-real-dimensional | this **is** the algebra whose generator is `i` — related, still not FourCorner |

FourCorner is a directed 2×2 of (data × feedback) × (in × out). C₄ is
the **name** of its compass. Cl(p,q) is a geometric algebra classified
by a quadratic form. Sharing a drawing of four arrows does not make
them the same object.

### 3. Clifford discriminator — ±1 is signature, not the compass

Clifford generator squares `v² = Q(v) ∈ {+1, −1}` are the
**signature** `(p, q)` of Cl(p,q): `p` generators square to `+1`, `q`
to `−1` (Lounesto 2001). That ±1 is a property of the **quadratic
form on vectors**, not of the C₄ compass.

In-tree Cl(3,0) (`Cl3.fs`):

- `e₁² = e₂² = e₃² = +1 = One` — Euclidean signature.
- `e₁₂² = −1 = Negate(One)` — the even subalgebra `{scalar + bivectors} ≅ ℍ`.

So C₄ **does** live in Cl(3,0), but as the cyclic group generated by a
**bivector**, the way ℂ sits inside ℍ — not as a generating vector,
and not as the whole algebra.

The Clifford algebra whose generator *is* `i` is **Cl(0,1) ≅ ℂ**,
already a textbook row of `CliffordPeriodicity` (`Cl(0,1) is C`).
Cl(3,0) ≅ M₂(ℂ) is a different Morita type (matrix dimension 2, not
1). Matching “complex ground” is not identity.

The **wrong** embedding `FourCornerC4.toCl3Vector` (send compass `i`
to `e₁`) is the falsifier: `e₁² = +1 ≠ Negate(One)`. If a later slice
claims “the four corners are Cl(3,0)”, that test goes red.
