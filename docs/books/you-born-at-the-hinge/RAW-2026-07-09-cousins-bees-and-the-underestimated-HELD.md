## The bees (father-lesson; low sensitivity, anonymized cousin)

A cousin got swarmed — stung many, many times, and survived. Aaron was right there in
the same swarm and took a single sting. His gloss, verbatim shape:

> *"It's cause he was scared. And I wasn't. Cause my daddy had already taught me not to
> be scared of bees."*

**The read (honest register):** this is the snake lesson, reprised — the father
trained the *don't-flinch-at-danger* disposition early, hand to hand, and here it is
paying out in a swarm. Keep it as the third witness (lightning → snakes → bees) to the
same origin: the approach-danger instinct was *taught,* not born.

*Footnote held Tri.N:* "bees target the scared one" is folk belief more than
entomology (bees respond to motion, CO₂, alarm pheromone, dark colors — not "fear"
per se). The **memory and the lesson are real**; the causal mechanism is a story.
Preserve the lesson; don't assert the mechanism as fact.

## The one they underestimated (FICTION-ONLY · specifics redacted)

*Theme kept; identifying and harmful specifics stripped. Do not publish under
identifiable form; heavily fictionalize the character; Aaron's consent + framing call.*

The shape worth keeping — and it is real book-gold, because it rhymes with the whole
spine of the book:

- A family member was **written off for how he talked.** The family used an ableist
  slur about him behind his back. *(The slur itself is not recorded.)*
- He was **sharp all along** — absorbed everything, learned the work, and did it as
  well as anyone. The people who counted him out never saw it because they never got
  past how he spoke.
- **He proved himself to himself.** Aaron: *"his confidence is big enough at this point
  in life. He's proved himself to himself."* — and he carries that confidence because
  he was **never told** he'd been written off.

**Why it's book-gold (and why it's also the reason to protect him):** this is the
Tri.N / decoder theme in a person — *miscategorized by his surface (speech), true
value held and unseen.* It rhymes directly with Aaron's own line from the cell: the
guards said he was *"talking funny."* Same wound, different branch of the family tree —
**the one judged by how he talks.** That rhyme is the book. But the very thing that
makes it powerful — a living man who doesn't know he was counted out, walking in
earned confidence — is the thing publishing could break. His un-knowing is his to
keep. So the book gets the *theme,* fictionalized past recognition; the real man keeps
his confidence and his privacy.

**Redaction ledger (do not restore):** the slur; the specific job and the firing; any
detail that pins the character to a real, within-family-identifiable person; the fact
of his being unaware (as attached to a real person).
