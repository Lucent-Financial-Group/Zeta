## Timestamped study index

This index records the portions relevant to Zeta without making the analysis
depend on the supplied transcript remaining available.

- **15:53-17:03:** quantum theory is presented as a decision manual used by an
  agent, not a theory that defines what an agent is.
- **20:30-21:14:** the participatory-universe framing replaces a fully detached
  observer with a relation between observer and observed.
- **23:44-26:23:** a quantum state is treated as an agent's assignment; removing
  the agent removes that assignment from the account.
- **25:03-25:41:** measurement is reframed as an action whose encounter yields
  an experience for the acting agent.
- **42:44-45:05:** regularity is described as abstraction over distinctions,
  while individual events retain novelty.
- **46:34-49:18:** Fuchs discusses neutral monism: the basic constituents are
  neither intrinsically mental nor material, acquiring those descriptions
  through relations. William James and Alfred North Whitehead are named.
- **49:18-52:56:** the discussion moves toward pure experience,
  pan-experientialism, and the still-open question of what constitutes an
  agent.
