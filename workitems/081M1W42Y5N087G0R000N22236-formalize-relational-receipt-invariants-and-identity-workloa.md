# Formalize relational receipt invariants and identity workload multiplier

Preregister and execute the bounded [relational identity lane](../docs/research/relational-identity/README.md).

Acceptance requires a typed receipt kernel with an explicit invariant on a
declared disclosed cut; separate missing coverage, invalid authentication and
contradiction; an additive entropy-floor premise and pairwise workload
multiplier with dimensional accounting; and exact independent replay of
transport, adversary, entropy and topology controls. Retain the coherent
single-controller witness that prevents a Sybil-soundness claim.

Runtime identity policy, persona memory and new cryptographic primitives are
outside scope. The [protocol](../docs/research/relational-identity/2026-09-06-protocol.md)
must be pushed before collecting outcomes.
