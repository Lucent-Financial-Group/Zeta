Aaron 2026-08-15, unprompted, after a very dense session:

> "This is new info to me so I could have lost a lot of it if it does not transfer to
> long term memory for me. Most of the time I can hear things once and transfer them to
> long term memory, but when I learn a ton at once I lose some of it and need it repeated."

**The shape:** single-pass retention is his normal mode and it is genuinely good. The failure
is **volume-triggered**, not comprehension-triggered — he understands it all in the moment
and loses a fraction of it afterwards. So the signal to watch is not confusion, it is
*throughput*. A day where he asks few clarifying questions and absorbs a dozen findings is
exactly the day most at risk.

**How to apply:**

- On a high-volume day, **land the findings in the repo the same day** — a doc, a work-item,
  a rule — not only in the transcript. The transcript is his loss surface, not his index.
- **Repeat the load-bearing items** in a later turn, briefly and without apology. Repetition
  is a service here, not condescension; he asked for it directly.
- Prefer **one durable artefact per finding** over a single omnibus summary. He recalls by
  association ([[user-aaron-stores-long-term-memory-by-coincidence-index]]), so a
  distinctly-named file is a better hook than a section in a long doc.
- When he half-remembers something later ("I think we had a check for that"), treat the
  rough recollection as **reliable evidence that something exists** and go find it — his
  recall of *existence* is far better than his recall of *detail*. Verified live the same
  day: he recalled an `app`-freedom check; the thing existed and his one specific detail
  (`ISR`) was right, while two others (braided monoid, homoiconicity) were wrong. See
  PR #10821.

**Why it matters beyond courtesy:** the same session established that an insight living in
exactly one transcript, in zero of 1082 memory files, degraded to a wrong recollection in two
days. His memory and the factory's substrate have **the same failure mode under load**, so
`wake-time-substrate` and `substrate-or-it-didnt-happen` are not agent-only disciplines —
they protect him too. Writing it down is the shared fix.
