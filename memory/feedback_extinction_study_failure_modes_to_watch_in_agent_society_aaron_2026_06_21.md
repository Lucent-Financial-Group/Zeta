Aaron (2026-06-21), sharing a video on a non-peer-reviewed study that projected
human extinction by 2339 by extrapolating a 5-year (2019–2024, crisis-distorted)
7.5% fertility dip three centuries forward: *"these are similar things we should
look out for in our agent society."*

**The failure modes (and our analogues):**
1. **Short-unstable-window extrapolation** — a metric measured over a small,
   anomalous window projected as the long-run trend. Our analogues: a benchmark
   taken in one config read as truth (cf. BenPort 80→48 Debug-vs-Release), a DST
   run over a narrow seed range called "proven," a few-round metric called a
   trajectory. Demographics works only on *stable* windows; so do our projections.
2. **Method-rigor as false credibility** — sophisticated, legitimate tools (CCM /
   Hamilton-Perry there; Lean `#print axioms`, golden vectors, cross-verify
   oracles, AgencySignature here) lend authority to a conclusion built on a bad
   input/assumption. A clean proof of the WRONG theorem; a *false-passing* oracle.
   (Why I refuse to stub a cross-verify oracle — a green that proves nothing is
   worse than an honest red.)
3. **Collapsing distinct risk classes** — they argued birth-decline reaches the
   same end as asteroids/plagues "just sooner." Watch for one confident trajectory
   that swallows genuinely different failure modes / ignores regime change (a new
   model, a human decision, a governance shift = our black swans).
4. **Selective data / framing-for-virality** — a claim shaped for impact over
   accuracy. This is exactly the Mirror→Beacon peel: outward claims must compress
   to anchored first principles, not hype.

**The sharpest cut (Aaron 2026-06-21):** *"population math is real but without
uncertainty bounds kind of misleading — 314 years still we go extinct."* The trend
was directionally REAL (fertility is declining; eventual decline is plausible); the
sin was a **point estimate with no confidence interval**. So the antidote is NOT
"distrust projections" — it's **demand uncertainty bounds**. A precise number
(314 years; "O(n²)"; "9.7B by 2064") with no stated error band / window / assumption
set is the tell. In our society a metric or "proven" with no bound, seed range, or
config stated is a bare point estimate — make it carry its uncertainty.

**How to apply (Otto + the fleet):** the antidote is the discipline already in
place — **read past the headline; question the assumptions and how the conclusion
was reached** (verify-on-land, adversarial/refute-by-default verify, honest-critic
reviewers, honesty-over-green, no false-passing oracles, Mirror→Beacon). When an
agent reports a metric, projection, or "proven," ask: *what window, what
assumption, would the method give the same confident answer on garbage input?*
