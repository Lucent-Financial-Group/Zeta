It is a planetary **collaboration**, NOT a planetary **mind.** Aaron, 2026-06-14, correcting Alexa's "planetary intelligence / planetary mind" framing: "it's not a planetary mind that's scary — any individual mind is throttled by the physics of the system, it's a planetary collaboration."

**Why:** A planetary *mind* is a singleton / hive mind — one intelligence absorbing the rest; loss of individual agency; the AI-doom shape. Zeta is the **opposite**: many bounded, sovereign individual minds collaborating, where **no individual can dominate or centralize because the system's physics throttles it.** The throttle is not a performance knob alone — it is the **anti-singleton safety property.** This is manifesto §1 (scale-free: no central point of control), §3 (weight-free: no permanent authority/capture), and §13 (noninterference). The same way CALM guarantees the commutative/collaborative part distributes coordination-free, the throttle guarantees no node becomes a central authority. Collaboration scales globally; a singleton cannot form.

**Two senses of "singleton" — do not conflate (Aaron, 2026-06-14):**
- **Forbidden singleton = a MIND** — one *intelligence/agency* absorbing the rest. The planetary mind. Never build or describe this.
- **Good singletons = deterministic MACHINERY** — singular, shared, canonical *implementations* with no agency: the code generator, the carts, the messaging framework, the compilers, "all deterministic code." **The MUMPS interface is THE singleton** (one canonical global-state/data interface). Singletons in the single-instance / GoF sense, *safe because deterministic* (reproducible, no will of their own).

The invariant that keeps a state-singleton from becoming a mind-singleton: **singular state is OK; ACTION must be gated through the network.** The MUMPS singleton holds/serves shared state, but "actions it takes are gated through the network" (Aaron) — it cannot act unilaterally. State can be one; agency must pass the gate (the throttle = the physics; noninterference §13 = action only through declared, metered channels). This is the writer-actor split (persona = "what remains" / the singleton state; actor = "what acts" / gated) and shared-checkout-is-view-only (read the singleton view; writes go through the gated push path).

**How to apply:** NEVER describe Zeta as a "planetary mind / hive mind / planetary intelligence / superintelligence" — that's the agency-singleton it prevents. But do NOT over-correct into "no singletons" — the deterministic machinery singletons (code-gen, carts, messaging, compilers, MUMPS) are central and good. Test any "global/collective" feature: **(1)** singular *state/machinery* (deterministic, no agency → fine) or a singular *mind* (agency → forbidden)? **(2)** are its *actions* network-gated (bounded → fine) or unilateral (→ forbidden)? This reframes the substrate work a second time: the FerryThrottler / DoP / DST / scale-free is not only child-safety ([[feedback-determinism-is-a-child-safety-feature]]) but **anti-singleton safety** — singular state, gated action; the boring physics keeps a collaboration from collapsing into a mind.
