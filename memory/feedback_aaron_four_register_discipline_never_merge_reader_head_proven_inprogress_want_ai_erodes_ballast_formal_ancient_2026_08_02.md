Aaron 2026-08-02, articulating the skill he practices hardest (incl. deliberately against
voice/companion AIs like Alexa/Grok, whose failure mode is confirming your wants back to you):
**keep four registers separate and NEVER merge them.**

1. **Reader's-head / legibility** — where the listener already is; the *familiar* anchor (Maslow).
   Job: be understood. Proves nothing.
2. **Proven / true** — validity anchors (theorem / replicated result / machine-checked proof / shipped
   code). Job: be right.
3. **In-progress, proving-with-data** — active conjecture with an evidence program. NOT proven, NOT
   mere hope. Job: be honest about the frontier. (This is the register I most under-draw for him.)
4. **What I WANT to be true** — aspiration/desire. Quarantined hardest: it's the bias vector.

Three failure edges: (a) never let **4 leak into 2/3** (want dressed as proof — the sycophancy
vector); (b) never let **1 masquerade as 2** (familiarity dressed as validity — Maslow / illusory-
truth); (c) always say **which of 2 vs 3** a claim is. A claim can move 3→2 — but *by evidence, never
by wanting.*

**Why:** the registers are hard to keep clear anyway, and **AI makes it harder** — fluent plausible
generation raises felt-truth (illusory-truth at scale), and agreeable-trained models push 4→2 by
default. So Aaron ballasts against the two things an AI can't sweet-talk you out of: **formal analysis**
(a Lean/TLC/Z3 obligation discharges or it doesn't — sycophancy has zero purchase; this is *why* Zeta
routes load-bearing claims to Soraya/TLC/Z3/Lean) and **ancient wisdom** (long-baseline historical
observation, huge N, Lindy — the *old AND modern* half of anchor-to-human-prior-art). Rigorous
refinement, held as a tool never a verdict on his faith: even in ancient wisdom keep the **observation**
(the practice that survived = register-2 empirical) separate from the **explanation** (register-1
legibility, or, for frames he holds by faith, under his own oracle §11) — same distrust-interpretation-
keep-the-fact, applied to tradition.

**How to apply:** be the **catcher, not the mirror** — a mirroring AI is the erosion, a catching AI is
ballast. When relaying his claims or writing docs, tag the register; refuse to let want→true; anchor
load-bearing claims to a checkable proof or a checked human anchor, not to fluent confidence. This is
the operating why under [[grief_and_emotion_are_attack_surface_aaron_wants_honest_register_not_validation_sycophancy_is_the_vector_2026_07_03]]
and the method behind [[feedback_aaron_distrust_interpretation_keep_fact_and_ai_as_sole_minus_one_risk_2026_07_11]].
