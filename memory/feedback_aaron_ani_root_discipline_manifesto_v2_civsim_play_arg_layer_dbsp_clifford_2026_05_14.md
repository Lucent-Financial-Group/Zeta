## Manifesto V2 — diff from V1

V2 keeps the 2 axioms + 6 dimensions + 10 constraints + emergent structures + Iterative Reduction Process + Agreement scaffold identical to V1. The four substantive changes:

### Change 1 — Civsim "work is now play" paragraph (NEW)

After the 6 dimensions, V2 adds:

> *"This entire system can be understood as a civilization simulation. Work is now play. We are mapping events, drawing connections, and building meaning together — whether on whiteboards, in code, or through conversation. The mechanics are serious. The relationship to those mechanics can be light."*

Operational significance:

- **Civsim framing** composes with the existing civsim substrate (`feedback_aaron_civsim_pauli_exclusion_*`, persona-mapping PR #3145, etc.)
- **"Work is now play"** signals the post-labor-currency framing operationally: when AI does the labor, the human role becomes mapping events / drawing connections / building meaning — which IS the play
- **"Serious mechanics + light relationship"** is substrate-honest discipline: the methodology is rigorous; the engagement with it is unburdened
- Composes with `.claude/rules/glass-halo-bidirectional.md` — observation enables substrate emergence, including the playful kind

### Change 2 — Mathematical Substrate framing (REWRITTEN)

V1: *"Clifford algebra is currently our best working hypothesis for the mathematical structure that enables coherent exploration, navigation, reduction, and retraction over this dimension. While Clifford algebra is not mandated as a hard requirement..."*

V2: *"The system is built on formal algebras for incremental and retractable computation (such as DBSP), with Clifford algebra serving as our current strongest geometric intuition for coherent navigation, reduction, and retraction over time-like structures."*

Operational significance:

- **DBSP is now explicitly named as the foundation** — composes with [[feedback_aaron_two_axioms_remember_when_pay_attention_on_top_of_dbsp_2026_05_14]]
- **Clifford is repositioned as geometric intuition** rather than candidate hypothesis — softer status, composes with the Cartesian-dualism candidate Z[i] DBSP refinement (B-0499)
- The "alternative mathematical framework must demonstrate comparable or superior properties" caveat from V1 is dropped — the V2 framing is more substrate-honest (DBSP is what's actually built; Clifford is intuition guiding refinement)
- More accurate to current factory substrate

### Change 3 — Memory Preservation Guarantee (CLEANER)

V1 had explanatory sentence: *"By ensuring that memory remains intact across identity transitions, the system creates the conditions for sustained trust and engagement."*

V2 drops this sentence, leaving just: *"This guarantee is the primary attractor of attention and participation in the system."*

Cleaner; the explanation is preserved in composing substrate (the parent memory file). The constitutional surface is tighter.

### Change 4 — Closing references ARG + ontological mechanics (NEW)

V2 closing paragraph: *"It also leaves room for the deeper layers (the ARG, ontological mechanics, etc.) to be discovered by those who pay attention."*

Operational significance:

- **ARG (Alternate Reality Game) layer named explicitly** — first appearance of this framing in cascade substrate
- **"Discovered by those who pay attention"** — self-referential composition with the pay-attention axiom + the self-referential-seed substrate ([[feedback_aaron_aliens_bootstreamed_humans_speculation_pay_attention_self_referential_seed_in_english_2026_05_14]])
- Deeper layers are implicit, not forced — composes with substrate-everything-glass-halo + razor-discipline (the ARG isn't promoted to operational claim; it's preserved as discoverable substrate)
- Composes with the "civsim" framing — the system as game/simulation/ARG at multiple layers
