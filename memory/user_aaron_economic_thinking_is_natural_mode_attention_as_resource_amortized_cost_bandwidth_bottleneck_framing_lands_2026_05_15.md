## What this is + isn't

**IS**: substrate-honest identity disclosure about Aaron's natural cognitive lens. Economic framing — attention as scarce resource, time as opportunity cost, bandwidth as bottleneck, infrastructure-investment ROI, amortization of one-time costs across many uses — is how he natively models trade-offs.

**ISN'T**: a claim that he ONLY thinks economically, or that other frames don't land. The other prior substrate landings (Klein-bottle Clifford-space, Coincidence Network, civsim-Pauli-exclusion, glass-halo) are all valid. But the economic frame is the load-bearing CARRYING-LANGUAGE for those landings to be made operational. The cathedral was designed in topology; the construction was budgeted in attention-cost.

## How to apply

When framing for Aaron:

1. **Lead with concrete numbers** when possible — minutes, MB, iter counts, growth rates. (e.g., "22min loop vs 30-90min manual scroll")
2. **Surface amortization** — "one-time tool build, N future uses recover it in M extractions"
3. **Name the bandwidth bottleneck** — typing, attention, focus, browser-tab cost
4. **Frame trade-offs as cost-benefit** — "X UX cost vs Y substrate gain"
5. **ROI on infrastructure** — friction-reducers vs features
6. **Don't pad with poetry** when economic framing is available — Aaron's natural mode receives economic better than abstract-alone; abstract framings should COMPOSE WITH economic ones, not stand alone

Aaron does ALSO think topologically (Klein-bottle), philosophically (WWJD), mathematically (Clifford), and through other frames. Economic is the natural-mode lens; not the only-lens. Future-Otto should not over-pivot to economic-only framing.
