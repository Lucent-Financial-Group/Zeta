Aaron 2026-08-19, giving his version of the repeated-game strategy:

> "tit for lesser tat, teach, play is my version to continue the infinite game"

## Each term does distinct structural work

| term | function | failure it prevents |
|---|---|---|
| **tit for LESSER tat** | damping | pure TFT echoes forever under noise — one mistaken defection starts endless mutual retaliation. Retaliating *less* than proportionally makes loop gain < 1, so it converges instead of oscillating. Axelrod's follow-up work found forgiving variants beat strict TFT in noisy environments; any real fleet is noisy. |
| **teach** | transmission | **the anti-babel operator** — keeps agents *reconcilable* while they diverge. Absent from Axelrod's tournament entirely: strategies there could reciprocate, never transmit. Adding it changes the game from *selecting* cooperators to *growing* them. |
| **play** | exploration | **the decorrelation source** — novelty under no obligation to win. Novelty is the non-buyable good (see the day-0-raid thread: primacy is buyable, novelty is not). |

**The structural point: each of the two scarce goods gets its own operator.**
*Play produces decorrelation; teach preserves reconcilability;* lesser-tat keeps the
game running long enough for either to matter. That maps one-to-one onto the arc's
two failure modes — everyone still the seed, and unreconcilable divergence
([[zeta-arc-is-decorrelation-from-s4-seed-without-babel]]).

## Anchors

**Carse, *Finite and Infinite Games* (1986)** — finite games are played to win and
end; infinite games to continue play. A real strategic claim, not a mood: an infinite
game changes what counts as a good move, because moves that win decisively but end
the game score badly. Which is exactly why lesser-tat beats proportional retaliation
here — proportional response is optimal for *winning an exchange* and terrible for
*continuing one*.

Also: Axelrod (evolution of cooperation, and the noisy-environment follow-ups on
generous/contrite TFT), the folk theorem, Ostrom (already cited in
[[every-bug-has-economic-value]]).

## Register

**`unmetered`.** This is a well-reasoned strategy, not a measured one. That
lesser-tat + teach + play dominates *in our environment* is untested until something
in the fleet runs it against alternatives. Good DST candidate — strategies against a
common seed are precisely what deterministic simulation is for.
