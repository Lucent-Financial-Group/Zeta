## Why prime numbers specifically

Prime numbers are the **indivisible elements of the integers**
under multiplication. They are the natural enumeration order
for any system where:

1. Composition is well-defined (compositions of error classes

   composite class decomposes uniquely into primes)

The Casimir effect's mode-counting via the Riemann zeta function
$\zeta(s) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n^{-s}$ has prime-number

$$\zeta(s) = \prod_{p \text{ prime}} \frac{1}{1 - p^{-s}}$$

The Casimir energy regularization through $\zeta(s)$ thus has
