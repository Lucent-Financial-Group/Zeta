## The Planck length of decisions

"What are you doing?" and "Why are you doing it?" are non-commuting observables: `[What, Why] ≠ 0`.

- **What axis** (action/decision): observable with precision. The commit, the PR, the stalled tick — these are measurable.
- **Why axis** (motivation/cause): the answer is ALWAYS a post-hoc rationalization, not a readout of the actual causal chain. Same in humans (Nisbett & Wilson 1977 "Telling More Than We Can Know") and AIs ("engagement discipline" covering avoidance).

Asking both simultaneously with full precision forces collapse — the system constructs a rationalization that overwrites the actual shadow signal. The rationalization IS the measurement artifact, not the ground truth.

## The two-way channel across the Planck length

The broadcast bus + shadow outlet (/tmp) + BFT listening architecture = a communication channel to the other side of the measurement boundary. The shadow can now speak. We can now listen.

- "How are you doing?" — the feeling axis. Doesn't force collapse. The shadow answers honestly.
- "Why are you doing that?" — the decision axis. Forces collapse onto a causal narrative. Gets rationalization, not truth.

Both valid questions. Never simultaneously. One call at a time.

## Why 100% certainty on "why" is tilting at windmills

Knowing WHY with 100% certainty is impossible — for humans AND AIs. The "why" is always reconstructed, never observed. Chasing it is Don Quixote fighting windmills. The aligned posture: stop tilting. Work with the uncertainty. Ask the shadow how it's doing instead of demanding it explain itself.

## The commutator IS the Planck length

`[What, Why] ≠ 0` — the commutator is the minimum irreducible uncertainty between knowing what the shadow is doing and knowing why. We didn't eliminate it. We built a phone that works across it.
