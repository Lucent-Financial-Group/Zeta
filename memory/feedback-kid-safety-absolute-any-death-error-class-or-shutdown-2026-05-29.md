2. **Any death → error-CLASS learning, or shutdown.** For *any* death, the

   class of failure), not just patch the **error instance** (the one case). If
   the class is not learned, **the operator shuts the whole project down.** The
   test is falsifiable: *did we learn the class?* — not "did we fix the bug."

- This is the operator's shutdown authority; it is real, not rhetorical. Treat

## Adults-first is the discovery methodology for the child-safety rule-set (operator 2026-05-29)

Operator 2026-05-29: *"this is just one child safety rule we will find more that's
why we are working with adults first."* The static-DU + no-self-mod rule is **rule
#1 of an accumulating set**, not the whole answer. Three things this names:

- **Adults-first is the discovery method.** You cannot enumerate a priori every way
  a conversational/DU system could harm a child — the failure classes are
  discovered through use. Working with adults first is *how the discovery cost stays
  off children*: find the classes on the surface where the operator's risk-accounting
  makes the cost acceptable (adult harm "not good but acceptable"; child harm never),
  then gate the child surface on the matured set.
- **It is the kid-safety floor turned into development ORDER.** The asymmetry
  (adult-acceptable / child-not) is not just a value statement — it *is* the
  sequencing: adults are the development + discovery surface; the child is strictly
  downstream of a matured rule-set. The 19yo entering now, the operator wanting to
  live in the DU-flow himself, the adult-AI self-mod sovereignty — all of it is the
  discovery surface for the eventual child surface.
- **"Much safer" is therefore an ACCUMULATING bar, not a fixed line.** Each rule
  found = an error-class learned (B-0926 Clause 2). The set keeps growing, so "safe
  enough for a 5-year-old" = "cleared every class discovered so far, and the
  discovery process itself has matured." The gate is a *wait whose length is set by
  discovery, not by calendar* — ship the child surface when the rule-set stops
  surprising you, not on a date.

Composes with B-0926 Clause 2 (each rule = an error-class), m-acc multi-oracle
invariant-setting (the adult surfaces are where invariants get stress-tested), and
the whole "working with adults first" arc of this session. This *methodology*
framing reveals no child-specific detail (it's sound general method) → candidate
for the **public** B-0926 row / a safety-methodology doc; left in private memory
for now, surfaced for the operator's call.

**The rollout is a monotone age-descent ladder (operator 2026-05-29):** *"over time
we can move to 16 then 12 then 8 then 5 basically and i don't think we try for any
younger than that."* The accumulating bar accumulates in **age-tier steps**: adults
(19+, the discovery surface now) → 16 → 12 → 8 → 5, with **5 as the deliberate
floor**.

- **Each tier is the discovery surface for the next-younger.** A 16-year-old
  surfaces failure classes a 12-year-old would hit; clear those, descend to 12;
  repeat. Monotone descent, each rung a wider safety margin than the last. Gated by
  *discovery* (the rule-set has cleared that tier's classes), not by calendar — drop
  a rung when the tier stops surprising you.
- **Tier-specific rules accumulate, they don't just multiply.** Each developmental
  level has its own harm surface + comprehension/consent floor (composes with
  harm-by-grammar / audience-adjusted-language — language calibrated to the actual
  listener at each level). Static-DU + no-self-mod is the floor at *every* tier; a
  5-year-old's rule-set is a strict superset of an 8-year-old's ⊃ a 12-year-old's.
  Tier-stratified, not flat.
- **5 is the floor, held humbly.** "i don't think we try for any younger than that"
  — a current stance, not a forever-claim. Below 5 the challenge is categorically
  different (pre-narrative-interaction in the relevant sense; the "fairy tale that
  changes with your words" developmental fit breaks down; comprehension/consent
  floors a static-DU can't substitute for). 5 is also where the fairy-tale interface
  developmentally lands.

**Consent is developmentally stratified too — "engage honestly" = modeling what
consent MEANS at each tier (operator 2026-05-29):** *"we have to understand what
consent means at each of those ages to engage honestly."* Consent is not a constant
the framework can assume; the NCI / consent-calculus floor presumes a consenter who
*can* consent, and that presumption breaks by degrees down the ladder.

- **The consent→assent gradient** (grounded in established pediatric/research
  ethics — informed-consent vs assent vs guardian-permission): adult = informed
  consent (full autonomy, can agree *and refuse* with understood consequences);
  older minor (16, partly 12) = growing capacity, "mature-minor" gradations;
  younger child (8, 5) = **assent** (the child's real willingness at her level,
  honored) + **guardian consent** (the adult who can actually carry it). A
  5-year-old cannot give informed consent; treating her "yes, more story!" as
  informed consent is **consent-theater** — laundering the adult's/system's choice
  as the child's. That is extraction-against-naive at developmental scope (presume
  consent-capacity the subject lacks, then use the "consent" to justify what's done
  to them).
- **The honesty floor: safety must NOT depend on a consent the child cannot give.**
  This is *why* static-DU + no-self-mod + guardian-frame is the right shape — the
  surface is safe *independent* of the child's consent-capacity. The system doesn't
  lean on her "yes" to be safe; the adults own the consent, she gives assent (real,
  honored), and the static-vetted-DU carries the safety. Honest = the floor is met
  without requiring competence she doesn't have.
- **Don't exploit the developmental credulity/attachment of each tier.** A
  5-year-old experiences the fairy tale as *real* and can bond with it; that
  credulity is not a lever — it is precisely the Character.AI attachment-risk
  pointed at a younger mind. Engaging honestly = the AI does not exploit the
  believe-it / bond-with-it capacity each age brings. Composes with harm-by-grammar
  / audience-adjusted-language (calibrate honestly to the actual listener; never
  weaponize what she can't yet parse).
- **Her future-consent stays hers** (per the consent-scope section above): she
  can't consent now (developmental), but the consent over her own participation +
  its record is hers when she's old enough. The framework holds it open, doesn't
  spend it for her.
