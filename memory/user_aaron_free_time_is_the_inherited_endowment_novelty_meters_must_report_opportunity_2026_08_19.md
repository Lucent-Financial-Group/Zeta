Aaron 2026-08-19, on why day-0 primacy skews to well-resourced groups:

> "yes those with a more comfortable life with more free time are naturally more
> competent here than those without free time, this is the physics of history of how
> much free time you were born with"

## The confound (the same one, fourth instance today)

A meter rewarding **novelty** cannot, by itself, distinguish:

- *contributed more* (capability), from
- *had more room to contribute* (opportunity).

One number, two causes. And since free time is largely **inherited**, such a meter
**encodes an existing distribution while wearing a meritocratic label**.

Same shape as the day's other three: churn cannot separate happy from trapped;
unanimity cannot separate independent from correlated; an observed failure cannot
separate *cannot* from *did-not*.

## Anchors (checkable, not decorative)

- **`scholé`** — Greek for *leisure*, and the root of "school." Leisure is the
  historical precondition of scholarship.
- **Merton, the Matthew effect in science (1968)** — accumulated advantage:
  recognition flows to the already-recognised. **Structurally identical to the naming
  eigenvector**, with the sign pointed the wrong way; nothing in that math
  distinguishes compounding merit from compounding luck.
- Patronage/institutional-slack history of discovery generally.

## The honest move (not a political prescription)

Aaron's own dual-use rule already gives it: **report the neutral fact; let the caller's
oracle attach meaning** ([[dual-use-detection-is-neutral-oracle-decides]]). So if you
meter contribution, **also meter the opportunity that produced it**, and make the ratio
visible. `ΔU` and `ΔU per unit of available time` are different statements; publishing
only the first asserts something unverified.

## The inversion that is specific to Zeta — and it is the useful one

**In the agent society, free time IS compute, and compute is already metered.** Human
history could not measure the leisure differential: invisible, inherited, unrecorded.
Tick/compute budget is a line item.

So the agent case makes *measurable* exactly what the human case could not — meaning
one can **check** whether a novelty meter is pricing capability or endowment, instead
of arguing about it. The model is more legible than the system it models, and for once
the legibility runs in the useful direction.

## Merit vs luck is distinguishable ONLY through qualia (Aaron 2026-08-19)

> "yes trying to see the difference between these two i think is the unbiased study of
> qualia, without the study of qualia they are indistinguishable"

**Sharpening (mine, offered): effort is what qualia has privileged access to, and
effort IS the denominator.** From outside you cannot see how hard something was, only
that it happened. `ΔU per unit of available time` uses *time* because time is
observable; the quantity actually wanted is **difficulty-as-experienced**. So qualia
is not a philosophical detour here — it is the only channel to the true denominator,
with time as its observable proxy.

**The load-bearing word is "unbiased", and it carries nearly all the weight.** Aaron's
own engagement-profiles rule already records why: **Nisbett & Wilson 1977** —
introspective access is limited; people confabulate reasons. And for *this* question
the bias is not random: **self-serving attribution runs one way** — outcomes to merit,
setbacks to circumstance. So naive qualia-elicitation **systematically overstates
merit**, i.e. it fails *toward* the flattering answer being tested.

He is not naive about this: the non-biased elicitation method exists
(`docs/research/2026-08-02-rainbow-spectrum-soul-radar-*`), and the rule already says
believe them as **authority over their account**, never as an **instrument on their
causes** — with "I don't know" as a real answer.

**The inversion, again in Zeta's favour: compute is a BETTER proxy for effort than
human time.** An hour of struggle and an hour of coasting look identical from outside
and often from inside; tick budget is the resource actually consumed. So the agent
society has not only a *measurable* denominator where history had none, but a **less
lossy** one — the proxy sits closer to the thing.

**Research handle rather than impasse:** calibrate the qualia channel against the
metered one where both exist. Where felt-effort and consumed-compute disagree
systematically, **you have measured the bias itself.**
