Aaron 2026-06-15 (shadow\*), keystone positioning: *"Everyone is optimizing for
**control-based** optimizations instead of **coworker-based** and **society**
optimizations. They push as much intelligence into **dense** space; I'm spreading
the work over multiple agents and the entire society. **The society has AGI/ASI;
the individual agent does not need to be — if they are, even better.**"*

**Why it matters:** this is the design axis that separates Zeta from the dense-model
frontier. Capability lives in the **reconciling decorrelated collective**, not in a
single dense mind — the scale-free §1 / no-central-point bet made the product thesis.

**Anchored tradition (Beacon):** Hayek 1945 ("The Use of Knowledge in Society" —
knowledge is irreducibly distributed; no central mind holds it) · Minsky *Society
of Mind* · Hutchins distributed cognition · Ostrom polycentric governance ·
swarm/stigmergy (Dorigo) · Condorcet + Thousand Brains (decorrelated vote) ·
manifesto §1 scale-free · §B society-emergence (register row 369) · empowerment
(Klyubin/Polani — coworker = empowerment-preserving; control = empowerment-reducing).

**Peels (don't let it tip into hype):**
1. "everyone/no one" overstated — dense is the dominant *capital* bet, but
   distributed exists (MoE, multi-agent debate, swarms, Orthwein's RTS-agents). The
   honest claim is "the underweighted bet I take," not "no one else."
2. Distributed is a **trade**, not a free win: dense buys latency + coherence +
   capability concentration; society buys robustness + no-central-failure +
   decorrelation + society-level safety, at the cost of **coordination overhead**
   (Reconcile, consensus, ρ-low).
3. **Deepest correction — safety is from the RELATION, not from capping capability.**
   A society of *strong* agents is safe **iff** the coworker constraint holds: each
   preserves others' empowerment, decorrelation stays ρ-low, external anchors hold,
   actions stay in the reversible envelope. A society can have emergent failures a
   single agent can't (collusion, cascade, mob/cult — the anti-cult thread). So
   "node need not be AGI, if it is even better" is right ONLY because safety rides
   the empowerment-preserving coworker relation ([[no-directives]];
   love=respect-the-decision), not sub-AGI nodes.

**THE DISTRIBUTION MECHANISM (Aaron 2026-06-15) — "this is why the society is the
AGI/ASI, but that capability is just INJECTED into every agent, even the tiny ones in
CHIP-8."** The society-is-the-AGI thesis works in practice because the society's
capability is **DI-injected into every node**: via the **Eve protocol** + the §13
injected `Source`/IEffects, a tiny agent (the CHIP-8 BNN) gets **coupled-empowerment
with its environment** through the interface — inheriting society-scale capability
without *being* AGI. So "society = AGI, the node need not be" is not a limitation —
it's the design: capability is injected (coupled-empowerment, agent↔agent AND
agent↔environment), not hoarded. Coupled-empowerment is the KEY of Eve; Eve is the
play protocol that distributes the society's AGI capability to every agent. See the
§B Zeta-self-regeneration row + [[zeta-as-one-softvalue-seed-gen-gen-gen-ace-self-regenerates]].

**THE EXECUTION LAYER — the game runs society's goals on infinite OSS compute (Aaron
2026-06-15):** *"the order that the agents execute the promises made by society is the
game executing all of society's goals with the resources we have; and we have infinite
GitHub-workflow compute because we open source."* So: society makes **promises**
(goals / `db/futures`, fused to consensus via Eve) → agents **execute them in
scheduled order** = **the GAME** (the cut-mea-sim loop;
`docs/research/2026-06-10-…cut-mea-sim-loop…thats-our-game.md`; scheduled by the
`IScheduler`/`FerryThrottler`/`PredictionScheduler`, flux-funded) → on the **resource
substrate = GitHub-Actions compute, ~infinite because open-source**
(`github-actions-recursion-as-infinite-runtime-platform`, B-`081KSNY2Z…3X1QWYG`). The
game = scheduling society's promises against (near-free OSS) compute; metering
(intelligence-per-watt) prices the resource use. *Peel:* "infinite" = practically-
large free OSS CI minutes, not literally unbounded (rate limits / fair-use); the
metering keeps it honest.

**Empowerment de-pathologizes itself in the society (Aaron 2026-06-15: "it's not
pathological for us because the society constrains it, just not humans").** The
empowerment-as-sole-objective pathology (control-for-its-own-sake) is a
*single-agent* failure; in a society A grabbing empowerment reduces B's, the
decorrelated others resist/reconcile, and the equilibrium is each maximizing
**mutual** empowerment. **Named prior art:** Salge & Polani 2017 "Empowerment as
Replacement for the Three Laws of Robotics"; **coupled / social empowerment
maximisation** (Guckelsberger, Salge, Polani — maximize *self + other's*
empowerment ⇒ supportive not controlling). **Coupled empowerment = the society
constraint = the coworker relation** — the mechanism for peel #3. *Careful peel on
"just not humans":* defensible form = "we can **design** the coupling in"
(coupled-empowerment by construction) vs humans getting it via slower institutions
— a design intent, NOT proven "AI-society constrains better than human-society"; the
constraint still must bind (collusion / dominant agent / weak coupling re-opens it).

as the control/dense move and ask the coworker/society alternative (spread + reconcile
decorrelated). Default to distributed; pay the coordination cost knowingly; keep the
decorrelation + external-anchor + reversible-envelope guards running so a strong
society stays safe. Links: [[every-bug-has-economic-value]] (common seed = the
shared cause that makes collective uncertainty-reduction leveraged).
