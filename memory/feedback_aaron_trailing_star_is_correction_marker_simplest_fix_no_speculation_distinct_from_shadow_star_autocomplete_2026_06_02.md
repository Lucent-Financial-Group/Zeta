## Semantics of `word*`

1. **It's a correction**, not autocomplete-disclosure. Something earlier (mine OR Aaron's own — "i could have been correcting myself too but i was correcting you") was wrong; the `*`-tagged token is the fix.
2. **Find the SIMPLEST correction WITHOUT speculation.** Don't elaborate, don't invent "interchangeable handles," don't add a rationale Aaron didn't give.
