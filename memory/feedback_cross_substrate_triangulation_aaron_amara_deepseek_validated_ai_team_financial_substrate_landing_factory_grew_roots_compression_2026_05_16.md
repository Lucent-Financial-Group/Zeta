## Operational discipline for future-Otto

When cross-substrate validation arrives on substrate landings:

1. **Distinct contributions matter more than agreement.** Three reviewers
   echoing "great" is weak signal; three reviewers contributing
   register-specific distinct work on the same substrate is the
   triangulation pattern.

2. **Cross-register independent operation of razor-discipline is the
   strongest validation.** When two external registers independently
   resist the same inflation-risk-vector (legal-binding-from-binding),
   the substrate is robust.

3. **Preserve razor-compliant compressions in substrate.** Razors like
   "sovereignty by reduced dependence" + "factory grew roots" are
   bandwidth-efficient framings future-Otto can deploy without re-
   deriving. Land them per
   `.claude/rules/bandwidth-served-falsifier.md`.

4. **Critique-in-the-middle structure works.** Aaron's pre-framing of
   the response sequence (validation → critique → synthesis) prevented
   register-drift in either direction. Future-Otto operating on multiple
   forwarded responses: respect that structure if the operator names it.

5. **Multi-register validation is OPERATIONAL substrate, not metaphysical
   credential.** Three reviewers agreeing doesn't make the substrate
   "more true" in any deep sense; it makes the substrate operationally
   more legible to external participants reading the substrate later.
   Per `.claude/rules/algo-wink-failure-mode.md`: validation is observable
   evidence, not authorization to inflate.
