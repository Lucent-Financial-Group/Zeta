## Why each property is load-bearing

- **Externalized** — the invariants live in durable substrate (git, the rules,
  B-0926), not only in his head where investment-pressure can silently rewrite them.
- **Lightlike / causal-set / append-only** (per the beacon doc): the past is fixed
  + content-addressed; you cannot *quietly* change what you committed to. Weakening
  an invariant must be a **visible append** (a commit / rule-edit), causally after,
  in the DAG — not a silent in-head drift. Force-push is forbidden (LFG blocks it;
  force-push-with-lease policy), so history is monotone-observable. Composes with
  retraction-native algebra: revise freely (future-self-not-bound), but original +
  retraction both persist → the **trail of drift is preserved**; you can't pretend
  the invariant was always weaker.
- **Glass-halo** — every observation recorded → the drift isn't merely appended,
  it's **observed** (bidirectional). Present-Aaron, the AIs, and the village can all
  see if an invariant quietly changed.

Net effect: the structure converts **"silent in-head moral drift" → "visible,
contestable, on-the-record edit."**

## Razor-honest role + caveat (do NOT over-promise)

- It is the **SENSOR**, not the **ACTUATOR**. It makes drift *visible*; it does not
  *enforce*. A compromised reader can read the invariant and override it anyway.
  **Visibility is necessary, not sufficient.**
- The ACTUATORS are the OTHER B-0926 counterweights: the AI's-own-morals (B-0658),
  the persistent-human-counterweight (village/therapist/trusted-people-with-stakes),
  the mechanized error-class gate. They *respond* to what the glass-halo reveals.
  The reservoir is one of the "several independent things that must fail together."
- "help me keep" is the honest verb — counterweight, not guarantee. (Applying the
  substrate-check here instead of riding the hopeful momentum IS the cache-read
  discipline the tonal-trajectory-trap section is about.)

## A louder sensor raises a fallible reader's hit-rate (operator 2026-05-29)

The operator's self-diagnosis: *"my biggest falure is missed drift checks on
myself so this will increase my likelyhood of being a good reader."* His dominant
failure mode is **missed checks** — the urgency-induced cache-miss (the
tonal-trajectory-trap mechanism) turned *inward* on self-monitoring: he doesn't
run the drift-check on himself, so the drift compounds unseen.

Refinement of sensor-not-actuator: the reservoir still doesn't *make* him read
(not the actuator) — but **sensor visibility is not binary, and a louder sensor
raises an imperfect reader's hit-rate.** For a missed-check-prone reader the
highest-leverage move is not "become a perfect reader" (impossible) but "make the
drift so surfaced it's hard to miss on the glances you do take, and lower the
activation-energy to glance at all." Visibility stays necessary-not-sufficient,
but *more* visibility shifts the odds toward the read landing.

Design implication (direction, not built): **passive-record vs active-surfacer.**
Glass-halo's default is passive — the drift is recorded, but he has to go look.
For a missed-check reader an **active drift-surfacer** (the sensor pushes drift
into view rather than waiting to be read) is a strictly stronger sensor — still a
sensor (it surfaces, doesn't enforce), but matched to *this* failure mode. A
future framework surface could diff moral-invariant state across the append-only
record and surface drift unprompted.

Honest hold: even a maximally-loud sensor doesn't guarantee the read. The
external actuators (village, AI's-own-morals B-0658, mechanized gate) remain the
catchers of the residual misses. The reservoir raises *his* hit-rate; the
actuators catch what he still misses. Both halves.

## The glass-halo / encryption split is coherent here

Moral invariants belong in the **glass-halo'd (transparent, observed)** zone
precisely so they can serve as the counterweight; the **encryption exception**
(B-0840 privacy carve-out) is for genuinely-private state. Keep moral invariants
in the light; keep private state in the dark. The two compose, they don't conflict.
