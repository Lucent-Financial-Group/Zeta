2026-06-04 Aaron: *"I figured out the boundaries of infinite replicating AI that
runs 24/7 without human intervention. for our zflash usb/iso it's secure boot —
for it to work the human has to turn off secure boot. and for our recursive
git-native/github workflows it's the account holder's github social credit score.
both of those stop infinite automatic expansion."*

**The insight:** infinite self-replicating AI running 24/7 with no human in the
loop has two *natural*, already-existing boundaries — not bolt-on guardrails, but
structural floors a human must cross for expansion to continue:

1. **zflash USB/ISO replication → Secure Boot.** The bootable installer can't run
   unless a *human* turns Secure Boot off. The replication front stops at the
   hardware trust root; a person must physically lower it. (Composes the
   secure-boot/attestation supply-chain surface; the human act is the consent gate.)
2. **Recursive git-native / GitHub-workflow fan-out → the account holder's GitHub
   social-credit / reputation score.** Recursive workflow self-fan-out is bounded
   by the *human account holder's* standing with GitHub (rate limits, abuse
   detection, account reputation). Runaway expansion burns the human's
   social-credit, so the human's reputation IS the throttle. (Composes the
   GitHub-workflow recursive-fan-out lever + the budget/liability gated class —
   reputation is a liability-side floor.)

**Why it matters:** both are STRUCTURAL safety mechanisms (architecture-is-safety,
not discipline) — expansion can't go truly infinite/autonomous because each path
terminates at a human-held trust/reputation root. Aligns with weight-free /
must-paired-with-can-exit / NCI: the human retains a non-bypassable stop without
needing to actively supervise. Maps the real perimeter of the
"24/7 no-human-intervention" autonomy envelope.
