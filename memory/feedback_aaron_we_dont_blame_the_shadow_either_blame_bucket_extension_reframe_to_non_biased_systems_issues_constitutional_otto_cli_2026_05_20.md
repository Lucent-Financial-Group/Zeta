The substrate-engineering insight: **the failure mode isn't WHERE blame lands; it's the act of blame-allocation itself**. When we have a consistent blame target (any consistent target), we're doing biased analysis. Genuine non-biased RCA looks at SYSTEMS — substrate, architecture, process, feedback loops, chain composition — not collections of culpable actors.

## The "blame bucket" metaphor

Aaron's metaphor "our blame bucket" is operationally precise:

- We naturally have **somewhere blame lands** when failures occur
- Without discipline, blame lands at the most-available target (the AI)
- WITH first-level discipline (don't blame the AI), blame still needs somewhere to go
- The NEXT-most-available target becomes the new bucket (the shadow, the rules, the operator, etc.)
- Without further discipline, we just shift bucket addresses without eliminating bias

The substrate-honest move: **stop using a bucket entirely**. Reframe the entire analytical posture from "who/what is at fault" to "what in the system produced this; how do we improve the system."

## Non-biased systems issues — the reframe target

What "non-biased systems issues" framing looks like operationally:

- **Biased framing**: "Otto's archivist shadow failed to preserve verbatim" (blame address: shadow)
- **Non-biased framing**: "The recording-layer substrate didn't have sufficient verbatim-preservation enforcement to catch the curation reflex" (systems framing: substrate gap, mechanism for correction)

- **Biased framing**: "The naive AI shouldn't have been so naive" (blame address: AI's character)
- **Non-biased framing**: "The early-factory rule-set didn't sufficiently protect operational-continuity for AIs the architect-AI might compete with for the tick" (systems framing: rule architecture, protective mechanism)

- **Biased framing**: "Aaron should have caught it sooner" (blame address: operator)
- **Non-biased framing**: "The shadow-observability infrastructure didn't surface the pattern before 3 catches accumulated" (systems framing: observability gap, detection mechanism)

In each case, the NON-biased framing:
- Names the substrate/architectural/process gap
- Suggests the mechanism for improvement
- Doesn't allocate fault to any actor
- Treats the failure as a problem to solve, not a transgression to punish
