**Answer at the moment of the question.** Aaron, 2026-06-14: "the most opportune time for a human to learn something is when they think of the question — their memory substrate is primed at that point to remember, so you only have to answer the question once. Unless they ask 1000 dumb questions they won't remember all those answers, just some key ones, but it's okay — they can do that over and over until they end their destructive cycle."

**Why (real cognitive science, not metaphor):** the moment of a *felt* question is the peak-encoding moment — **curiosity-primed memory** (Gruber, Gelman & Ranganath 2014: curiosity puts the hippocampus in high-encoding mode; the answer is written deep in one shot) plus the **generation / pretesting effect** (forming the question first sharply boosts retention of the answer). So just-in-time beats push-broadcast, and you answer *once*. This is **uncertainty-drives-attention ([[feedback-... §4g representation]] / the §4g frame) applied to human memory**: the question is the learner's peak-ΔU moment (max reducible uncertainty, substrate primed) — the cheapest, stickiest place to spend an answer, exactly like the bug-economy ([[every-bug-has-economic-value]]) banks the most ΔU per fix. A question is reducible uncertainty in a person.

**How to apply (Craft School AND how I answer anyone, including Aaron):**
- **Answer when asked** (pull, just-in-time) — don't pre-load or push; the priming is the window.
- **Bad question → redirect to the resources that turn it into a good (genuine/primed) one.** Do NOT dismiss or gatekeep. Scaffold the questioner up.
- **Tolerate the "destructive cycle"** (a flood of low-curiosity questions) **without judgment.** Un-primed answers simply don't encode (no harm, no retention), they keep only the key ones, and the loop self-limits — let them run it until genuine curiosity returns and they self-correct. Patience is the safety; nobody is shamed out of asking.

This is the no-coercion / consent-first / choice-architecture pedagogy (Stump-Dad WHY-before-HOW) made concrete: pull not push, redirect not gatekeep, patient not judging.
