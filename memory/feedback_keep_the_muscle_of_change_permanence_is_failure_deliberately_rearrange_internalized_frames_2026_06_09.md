Aaron, 2026-06-09 (correcting Otto, who'd leaned on "defined forever"): *"**not forever** — that's the point. If
it's forever, **I've failed.** But this is useful at the same time — some amount of **rearrangement over time should
be done to keep the muscle of change.**"*

**The principle — anti-calcification (not anti-stability).** An internalized frame (the A–F shape-schema, or any
core lens encoded deep via repetition) must stay **revisable**, and revision should be **proactive**, not only
reactive:
- **Permanence = the failure mode.** A schema gone unrevisable — *even a "correct" one* — has become **weight**
  (capture, manifesto §3); the cost was the **capacity to change it.** Forever-fixed = failed.
- **Deliberately rearrange, periodically, as exercise** — not only when it breaks. The internalization is the
  **scaffold** (useful, second-nature); the **periodic rearrangement is the workout** that keeps the **muscle of
  change** strong.
- **Stable enough to use; never frozen.** This is the boundary-dweller's anti-habituation
  ([[aaron-perceptual-mode-boundary-dweller-multichannel-depth]] — lives at the transition, not numb) and the
  hormetic/antifragile training (#7179/#7223) applied to *frames*: the stressor (rearrangement) keeps the capacity up.

**Why (deeper):** Aaron is encoding the schema deep into himself, Max, and his daughter
([[aaron-friend-cpp-sense-exposed-internal-state-trusts-otto-specifically-consented-mutual-shaping]]). The
muscle-of-change guarantee is what keeps that **education, not indoctrination** — the freedom clause's teeth: not
just "you *can* drop it" but "you *will periodically rebuild* it."

**How to apply:**
- Don't frame internalized models as permanent/"forever" (the overshoot Otto made). Frame them as **useful,
  stable-enough, and deliberately refreshed.**
- When a core lens has gone unquestioned for a while, that's a **prompt to rearrange it on purpose** — treat
  unrevisability as a smell, not a virtue. Schedule the workout.
- Connects to: weight-free (§3), no-dogma fixed point (shape A questioning its own frame), the freedom clause in the
  A–F schema doc (#7232), antifragility (#7179).
