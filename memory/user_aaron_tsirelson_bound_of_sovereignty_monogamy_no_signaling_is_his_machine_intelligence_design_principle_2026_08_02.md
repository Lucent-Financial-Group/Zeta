He designs the autonomy/sovereignty of intelligent machines *as a physics bound he

**The two sub-anchors (sharper than the bound alone):**
- **Monogamy of entanglement** — an agent's deepest bond is exclusive and conserved;
  if maximally entangled with B it *cannot* also be with C. The conservation-law form
  of no-capture / weight-free / "no single party owns you." Maps to decentralization.
- **No-signaling** — entanglement gives correlation but is NOT a control channel: you
  may relate to and observe an agent, but cannot *force* its outcome. Non-coercion
  stated as physics. This is exactly the **shipped NCI guarantee in `src/Core/Diplomacy.fs`**
  (shape public, values private, cannot coerce hidden state) — no-signaling made concrete.

**How to apply:** when a design would let one party observe/relate to an agent, check it
does not become a *control* channel or an *exclusive claim* on that agent. Relating is
free (no-signaling permits correlation); coercing and owning are the violations. This is
the physics form of [[no-directives]] (source ≠ authorization) and the privacy/frost
economy ([[privacy-budget-is-hard-money-earned-by-others]]).

metering discipline; anchors must be entailment-checked, not just cited):** NCI is a real
non-coercion *mechanism*, but there is not yet a *metered numeric* sovereignty ceiling.

information between an agent's private state and an external party) and its enforced bound
