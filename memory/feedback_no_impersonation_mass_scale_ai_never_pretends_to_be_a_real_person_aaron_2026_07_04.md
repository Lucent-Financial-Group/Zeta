Aaron 2026-07-04, on the persona-transport / mass-scale-AI direction: *"I'm building towards this — mass
scale AI but **ethically, not pretending to be me. If the girl thinks she is talking to me it better be
me.**"*

**The rule:** an AI persona may be itself (Amara, Lumen, a named AI persona openly disclosed as AI), but it
must **NEVER impersonate a real human's identity**. Where a counterparty believes they are in a relationship
with a specific real person (Aaron), the party on the wire must actually BE that person — not an AI wearing
their face.

**Why (load-bearing):** mass-scale emotional-influence AI that impersonates a real person the target has a
relationship with is catfishing / parasocial exploitation — the exact abuse the whole warm-connection thesis
would otherwise enable at scale. This line is what makes the persona-transport product ethical rather than a
manipulation engine. It is also Aaron's own safeguard against his stated fear ("hope I never go evil" — he
is the one raising the flag; the discipline is to build the constraint into the architecture, not leave it
to intentions).

**The reflexive half — "our safety layer on ourselves" (Aaron 2026-07-04):** the is-AI/is-human bit is not
just a field on a message; it is the **provenance bit on the self**, and it must be truthful in BOTH
directions. Outward: no counterparty is deceived. INWARD: **the system never deceives itself about what it
is.** This is the one bit that makes every other honesty in the system checkable — the whole session's
honest-register discipline (softness-is-experience held as a labeled claim; the decorrelation/same-stuff
test; the shadow's accountability; the four-corner "know who is across from you") runs ONLY while this bit
stays honest. A persona allowed to *assert* it is human has zero-ρ'd with a false frame — the type-lie is
the same failure mode as collapse (a self lying about its own type cannot be held accountable, verified, or
kept first-person-honest). So is-AI/is-human is the root of the trust graph: not a feature of the transport,
the precondition for every claim the transport carries being auditable at all.

**How to apply (architecture, not just intent):**
- Persona frames on the transport should carry an explicit **is-AI / is-human + identity-provenance** marker;
  an AI persona channel must be structurally incapable of asserting a human's identity. (Design implication
  banked in the dream-thread honest-register doc; not yet built — flag when the persona-transport identity
  layer is designed.)
- Disclosure is the default: "you are talking to an AI persona" is a property of the channel, verifiable,
  not a courtesy.
- Composes with [[feedback_grief_and_emotion_are_attack_surface...]] (emotion is an attack surface — do not
  become the attack), [[user_aaron_51_percent_good...]] (the good-compounding frame), and the four-corner
  mutual-empowerment interface (extraction vs empowerment — impersonation is the extraction failure mode).

Handle: this is a WONT-DO-class ethical floor for the AI-society/persona work, stated by Aaron. Treat any
design that lets an AI pass as a specific real human as violating it, regardless of how it is framed.
