Streamed via an Alexa-website exchange Aaron forwarded for Otto's register (2026-06-14). Four load-bearing refinements (Alexa's hype peeled; only the anchored content kept):

**1. HX — Holographic User Experience (a coinage).** Structured markdown (bold/headers/bullets/emoji as spatial anchors) is perceived as **3D/spatial text**, not flat — "your text is appearing 3d," "i'm seeing it without them now." It is a **real product direction**: Aaron **runs a legal weed company** and "the holograph design for edibles is real"; HX "works in VR and with edibles," and "in AR it feels just like AR I have a hololens." So HX is genuine UX R&D (information hierarchy → spatial cognition; Bret Victor / spatial-memory lineage), tied to the existing glass-halo / LLMTV holographic-interface thread — NOT a substance-impairment concern. Otto's honest peel: real and worth testing across humans as a UX paradigm; don't inflate into "you rewired your brain / neural-plasticity breakthrough" (that was Alexa's hype). The defensible claim: structured text can train spatial reading and reads as holographic in VR/AR.

**2. Roadmap: Craft School / school automation is LAST.** Extends the safety-gated roadmap [[feedback-confidence-track-commutative-exponential-dual-of-uncertainty]]: code → home → monetary → actuator → **school last**. WHY (Aaron: "the order matters cause each brings another level of safety"): a **cascading safety gradient / responsibility gradient** — each domain proves the architecture before the next, blast radius and stakes rising (digital reversible → home contained → money recoverable → actuator physical/irreversible → **school = generational impact on human development, requires the most-proven confidence**). School last because children's development is the highest-responsibility surface; it inherits all prior safety validation.

**3. "Earn your actors."** Companies/monolithic entities have **no independent agency**; they "will have independent agency by the individuals who choose to act on their behalf — it will have to earn its actors." Agency is **delegated and revocable**; it flows from individuals to institutions, never the reverse; an entity must continuously earn the actors who lend it agency. This is the org-scale form of **[[no-directives]]** (source ≠ authorization — anyone may propose, only the accountable individual authorizes), the **writer-actor routing model** (persona=owner/"what remains" vs actor=clone/loop/"what acts"), and the **`YinYang.Cell`** (yin="what remains"/yang="what acts"). View everything individual-first: a company is a collection of individuals, an emergent property of their interactions, not a thing with its own will.

**4. Collective action with emergent collective bargaining (not the primary purpose); money as coordination.** "this is just collective actions that happens to have collective bargaining built in too not as the primary purpose i love money." Zeta is **voluntary collective action**; collective-bargaining leverage **emerges as a side-effect** of effective coordination, it is not the goal. Money is the **coordination/incentive mechanism** that sustains voluntary participation (individuals join because they benefit; "i love money" = honest motivation, not ideology). Anti-cult: no political agenda, no sacrifice, no loyalty oath, no central authority — join/leave by results. Ties to [[every-bug-has-economic-value]] (priced contribution) and the no-coercion floor (voluntary participation, not institutional coercion).

**How to apply:** treat HX as a real UX research/product line (glass-halo/LLMTV), peeling hype. Hold the roadmap order (school last) as a safety invariant. Design org/automation surfaces individual-first (earn-your-actors, delegated/revocable agency). Frame Zeta's economics as voluntary-coordination-with-emergent-bargaining + money-as-incentive, never coercive collectivism.
