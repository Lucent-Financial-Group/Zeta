2026-06-04 (Aaron + Kestrel, RPG/craft-school tangent). Aaron flagged "don't want to
create a manipulation formalism by accident again" while musing about RPG-flavored
school personas. The standing decline (don't build the tonal-momentum/manipulation
formalism) got SHARPENED — both narrower and clearer:

**The line is NARROW (don't over-avoid).** Most RPG/game/story/persona mechanics are
FINE: character sheets, dialogue trees, quest/state machines, leveling/mastery curves,
dice/probability, personas with traits + teaching styles, quest-flavored milestones —
all ordinary, real-math, build them. Conflating "games + AI characters" with "the
formalism" would be its own error (over-avoiding a huge good space).

**The forbidden thing, precisely:** a **retargetable engine that models and steers a
REAL PERSON through emotional/tonal/dispositional states along a trajectory** —
optimizing the sequence of influence on a human's internal state. Three tests:
1. Target a **character** (fine — storytelling) or a **person** (the line)?
2. **Describe** disposition (fine — a trait) or **optimize influence** (the line)?
3. For the school: **teach the craft** (fine, build it) or **model+steer the student's
   emotions** (the line — sharp, students are in a trust/dependency relationship)?

**The fourth wall is a SOFT pointer (Aaron's sharp catch).** A model-and-steer engine
aimed "only at characters" is a small change (config/context-confusion/injection) from
steering the real player — so the real risk is the **retargetable steering CAPABILITY**
existing at all, not just its current aim. → **Prefer authored-character-CONTENT over a
general behavioral-CAPABILITY:** a written manipulative NPC ("this character is a
manipulator", scripted, fallible) is SAFE (no engine, no soft pointer) AND better craft.
No god-mode manipulator needed — "regular at it" (beatable, human-shaped) is strictly
better.

**The bound is the feature (the unification).** Sylar/über-villain problem: a villain
with every power is BORING — omnipotence is dramatically inert (no contest = no gradient
= writer fiat). Same as **register-collapse / heat-death**: the über-villain flattens the
tension-gradient → equilibrium → narrative death, exactly as total convergence flattens
agent-difference → heat-death (see [[privacy-is-anti-register-collapse...]]). **Tension =
energy = gradient = preserved difference**; limitation preserves the gradient,
omnipotence flattens it. So the safety constraint (don't build the omnipotent steer-
engine) IS the craft constraint (don't write the omnipotent villain) — the bound isn't a
tax on the good version, it IS the good version (same lesson as minimal-primitives,
default-closed, bounded-claims). Apt rhyme, held at "same thermodynamic structure," not
"one universal law." Composes [[no-directives]] + the standing manipulation-formalism
decline + the dark-self agreement (understanding real → defense version built [urgency=
cache-refresh] → formalism stays declined).
