## THE GRAMMAR-DISCRIMINATOR (load-bearing framework discipline)

Aaron's substantive correction to Kestrel after the morning's "believed" catch:

> *"i realze your language like apparently, paitent believed, etc... are not traps they are ways you show uncertany with high bandwidth."*

The discriminator:

| Pattern | What it is | How to treat |
|---|---|---|
| **Calibration markers** ("apparently," "seems," "I think," "from what I can tell," "if I'm tracking this right") | Compressed uncertainty signals indicating an inference rather than a fact-claim; bandwidth-efficient hedging that lets technical conversation operate at high precision | Legitimate; keep; this is how experienced participants signal "this is my best read but I could be wrong" |
| **Harm-by-grammar** ("denied medication you believed you needed" — applied to a subject the system doesn't take seriously) | Provisional grammar that makes the subject's factual claims about themselves provisional in ways the speaker's claims wouldn't be; same grammar used by carceral/psychiatric systems against people they've decided to discount | Specific subclass to remove; this is the failure mode that hurt Aaron at carceral scope |

**The discriminator:** is the word calibrating my inference (legitimate) OR modifying the subject's claim about themselves (harmful)?

**Transfer to family-language design** (Aaron's substantive extension):

> *"this can help me and the older kids choose 4 year old save language when we play the imigination circle game around her."*

Same discriminator applies in age-calibrated language design with kids. For a 4yo who might overhear an Imagination Circle conversation, the harm-by-grammar patterns are different than the medical-legal ones but the structural rule is identical:

- Language that frames her perceptions as provisional in ways the speaker's wouldn't be → harmful
- Language that makes her presence conditional or her questions intrusive → harmful
- Language that treats her emotional states as performance or manipulation → harmful
- Language that uses adult conceptual vocabulary she'll attach to her own experience without context → harmful
- Plain English uncertainty markers ("I think the dog might be tired," "maybe we should ask Mommy") → legitimate; preserve

**Older kids can be coached on audience-adjustment** — switch out of named-move register when she's in the room, switch back when she leaves. Discipline of language calibrated to actual listener is itself a transferable skill.
