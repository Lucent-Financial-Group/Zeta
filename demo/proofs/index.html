Evidence before interpretation

This gallery reports finite computations, independent reproductions, fault controls, and negative results. A visual analogy is not a physical theorem. Agreement under shared code, seeds, data, or models is a conformance result unless independence is separately established. Unknown and conflict states remain visible rather than being collapsed into a marketing claim.

Current recommendation

Use content-addressed evidence union as replicated state, then run deterministic probabilistic queries over the complete canonical state. Do not use Gaussian product or pairwise covariance intersection as a CRDT merge.

RFFH · Architecture census

Unary blackboards, not a multi-variable heterarchy

The current reference-frame module instantiates two one-variable unary-factor blackboards with deterministic compensated posterior fusion. Parent and lateral links control admission and pose routing; they are not factor-graph edges propagating between columns. Extra graph rounds are inert in this unary arrangement.

Positive control: a separate two-variable equality-factor test shows the generic FactorGraph can propagate over a real edge. RFFH does not yet instantiate that structure.
